## English (U.S.)

**Subtitle (30 max):** Write your mornings into music

**Promotional text (170 max):**
Write a paragraph, press return twice, and hear it as music: choir, organ and bells tuned to your mood. Three paragraphs a morning. Nothing to sign up for.

**Keywords (100 max, commas, no spaces):**
journal,morning pages,diary,writing,mood,ambient,mindfulness,gratitude,calm,choir,streak,voice

**Description (4000 max):**
The Morning Frequency turns your morning writing into music.

Write a paragraph. Press return twice. The app reads the mood of what you wrote and plays a short, original piece of choir, organ and bells tuned to that mood, with a key, tempo and melody of its own. Write three paragraphs and your morning is complete.

WRITE OR SPEAK
• Type, or tap the microphone and talk. Say "new paragraph", or just pause, to hear your music.
• Name each entry. Everything saves automatically on your iPhone.

MUSIC THAT MATCHES YOUR MOOD
• 15 moods, from joyful and hopeful to melancholic and dreamy, including five inspired by Stevie Wonder and three in Spanish from flamenco and bolero.
• Turn the circle of fifths to choose a home key; each paragraph moves one step around the circle so your morning flows like a chord progression.
• Adjust reverb, echo, tempo, texture and volume.

A HABIT WORTH KEEPING
• A calendar of colored mood dots and a history of every entry.
• Streak sounds at 3, 5, 10, 14, 21 and 30 days in a row, plus a streak mix of every paragraph's music from your streak.

KEEP AND SHARE
• Save your writing as Word or PDF, and your music as audio.
• Add each day to one running Apple Notes note, with its own date and title.
• Make a short story video of your music for Instagram, TikTok or Facebook.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
• Text size buttons, an easy-read font for low vision, strong contrast and full VoiceOver support.
• English and Spanish.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your writing stays on your iPhone.

**What's New (version 1.0):** First release.

**App Review notes:**
No login is needed. To try it: turn the sound on, type a sentence in the writing box, and press return twice. A mood is chosen and music plays. Repeat three times to complete the morning, then tap "finish session" for the save, share, Notes and story video options. Voice input: tap "speak instead of typing" and say a sentence followed by "new paragraph". Everything is stored on the device; the app makes no network requests of its own.

The Morning Frequency convierte lo que escribe por la mañana en música.

Escriba un párrafo. Presione intro dos veces. La app lee el ánimo de lo que escribió y toca una pieza corta y original de coro, órgano y campanas, con su propia tonalidad, tempo y melodía. Escriba tres párrafos y su mañana estará completa.

ESCRIBA O HABLE
• Escriba, o toque el micrófono y hable. Diga "nuevo párrafo", o simplemente haga una pausa, para escuchar su música.

MÚSICA SEGÚN SU ÁNIMO
• 15 estados de ánimo, de alegre y esperanzado a melancólico y soñador, incluidos cinco inspirados en Stevie Wonder y tres en español, del flamenco y el bolero.
