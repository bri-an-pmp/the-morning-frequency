Smarter mood matching with Claude AI optional

What it does: on its own, the app guesses your mood from certain words like “happy” or “worried”, so writing without those words often gets a generic mood. With a Claude key, Claude AI reads your whole paragraph, so the music matches what you actually mean.

What it costs: about a fifth of a cent per morning, paid to Anthropic (the company that makes Claude), not to us. Anthropic asks for $5 of credit to start, which covers years of daily writing.

How to set it up (about 5 minutes):

  1. Go to console.anthropic.com and create an account.
  2. Open Billing and add $5 of credit.
  3. Open API Keys, click Create Key, and copy it (it starts with sk-ant-).
  4. Paste it below and tap save.
